Workshop on group dynamics

Montevideo, Uruguay · October 5–9, 2026

Venue: IMERL, Facultad de Ingeniería, Universidad de la República
Organizers: Juan Alonso, Joaquín Brum, Nancy Guelman, Nicolás Matte Bon, Cristóbal Rivas, Michele Triestino

About the workshop

The Workshop on group dynamics will take place in Montevideo, Uruguay, from October 5 to October 9, 2026.

The aim of the workshop is to bring together students, early-career researchers, and specialists working on group actions and related areas, including one-dimensional dynamics, symbolic dynamics, computability aspects of dynamical systems, and geometric group theory.

The program will include lecture courses, research talks, and informal discussion sessions.
